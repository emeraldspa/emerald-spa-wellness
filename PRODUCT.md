# PRODUCT

## Business

Emerald Spa & Wellness Centre. A day spa at Blackett Street No. 7, Windhoek
West, Windhoek, Khomas Region, Namibia. Woman-owned and indigenous-owned.
Open seven days.

## Objective

Turn search and social traffic into confirmed bookings on Fresha, and answer
the questions that stop people booking: what treatments exist, what they cost,
what the place looks like, who will treat me, and where is it.

## Audience

Residents of Windhoek and visitors to the city looking for massage, facials,
hydrotherapy, nails, lashes, or hair removal. Prices are read in Namibian
dollars. Most arrive on a phone.

## Primary conversion

Book on Fresha. Every page carries the action. Phone and WhatsApp are the
secondary paths for guests who prefer to talk first.

## Proof the business actually has

- 4.8 average from 228 verified reviews
- 130 bookable services
- 6 named professionals, each with an individual rating
- 6 photographs of the real premises
- Amenities: kid friendly, parking, near public transport, showers, lockers,
  bath towels, environmentally friendly, woman-owned, indigenous-owned

Nothing beyond this list is claimed anywhere on the site.

## Non-goals

No online payment, no account system, no blog, no newsletter, no contact form.
Fresha already owns booking and payment. Adding a second path would split the
conversion and create a data obligation the spa did not ask for.
